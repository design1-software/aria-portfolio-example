## What This Proves

Every case study in this portfolio passed a four-layer evidence pipeline:

1. **Deterministic checks** — empty, too-short, and placeholder submissions are rejected automatically.
2. **AI rubric grading** — each required-evidence item is scored against the lab's own checklist by an AI grader (satisfied yes/no, one-line reason, proving excerpt).
3. **Instructor review** — a human instructor reviews the evidence, the rubric results, and the student's work. Only instructor-approved labs appear here.
4. **Sanitization** — secrets and infrastructure identifiers are redacted before export. What remains is the student's real work, verified and safe to share.

No lab reaches this portfolio without instructor approval. No secrets leave the platform.

## How to Verify

Each case study ends with an **"Approved Note"** written by the reviewing instructor and the approval
date. The instructor's assessment is part of the artifact — it is not generated or templated.
